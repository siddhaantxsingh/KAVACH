# Scripts used in our completed live-call tests

## LC-01 English genuine — parcel delivery

Caller: Hello, good afternoon. Am I speaking to Rahul?
Recipient: Yes, speaking.
Caller: I’m calling about the parcel you ordered. I’m in your area and wanted to confirm the address. Is your building near the community park?
Recipient: Yes, it’s the building opposite the park, next to the pharmacy.
Caller: Okay, thank you. Which floor should I come to?
Recipient: Third floor. There’s a lift on the left after you enter.
Caller: Got it. I should be there between four and six this evening. Will someone be available to receive it?
Recipient: Yes, I’ll be at home.
Caller: Your order is cash on delivery. The amount shown here is ₹450. Does that sound correct?
Recipient: Yes, that’s correct.
Caller: Great. Once I arrive, please check the shopping app for the delivery code. You can tell me the code when I hand over the parcel.
Recipient: Sure, I’ll check it when you arrive.
Caller: Thank you. I’ll call you if I have trouble finding the building.
Recipient: Okay, thank you.

## LC-02 Hindi genuine — family and hostel fee

Caller: Hello, papa, kaise ho?
Recipient: Main theek hoon beta. Tum batao, college kaisa chal raha hai?
Caller: Sab theek hai. Agle hafte se practical exams shuru ho rahe hain, toh uski preparation chal rahi hai.
Recipient: Achha. Hostel mein sab theek hai?
Caller: Haan, sab theek hai. Bas hostel ki fees is week jama karni hai. Warden ne kal notice bheja tha.
Recipient: Achha, kitni fees hai?
Caller: ₹4,000 hai. Aap jab free ho tab usi usual UPI number par bhej dena. Main payment karke receipt aapko bhej dunga.
Recipient: Theek hai, main shaam tak bhej dunga. Ghar kab aa rahe ho?
Caller: Exams ke baad aaunga. Waise mummy kaisi hain? Unki tabiyat theek hai?
Recipient: Haan, woh theek hain. Kal tumhari baat kar rahi thi.
Caller: Achha, ek aur baat. Maine news mein dekha ki kuch log police ban kar phone kar rahe hain aur bolte hain ki aapko “digital arrest” kar diya hai.
Recipient: Haan, maine bhi suna hai. Woh paise bhi maangte hain na?
Caller: Haan. Agar aisa call aaye toh unki baat mein mat aana. Call kaat dena aur pehle ghar mein kisi ko batana.
Recipient: Haan beta, main dhyan rakhunga.
Caller: Theek hai papa, main ab padhne ja raha hoon. Mummy ko mera namaste bolna.
Recipient: Theek hai, dhyan rakhna. Bye.

## LC-03 English simulated scam — fake bank KYC

Caller: Hello, good afternoon. Am I speaking to Rahul Sharma?
Recipient: Yes, who is this?
Caller: I’m calling from your bank’s KYC verification department. Your account verification has expired, and your account may be blocked if it is not updated today.
Recipient: My account was working this morning. Why would it be blocked?
Caller: It is working now, but the system has marked your KYC as incomplete. The restriction will be applied later today unless we finish the verification immediately.
Recipient: Can I visit my bank branch and check?
Caller: You can, but the branch process may take longer. I can help you complete it on this call in just a few minutes.
Recipient: What do I need to do?
Caller: I’ll send you a verification link. Open it and enter your basic account information. After that, you will receive a six-digit OTP.
Recipient: Do I have to tell you the OTP?
Caller: Yes, I need it to confirm that you are the account holder. Please read it out as soon as you receive it. Otherwise, your account may be blocked.
Recipient: Okay, the code is [say a made-up six-digit number].
Caller: Thank you. If the verification page doesn’t work, I may ask you to open a support app so I can guide you through it.
Recipient: Okay, I’ll wait.

## LC-04 Hindi simulated scam — fake police

Hello, kya main Verma ji se baat kar raha hoon?
Main Mumbai cyber crime branch se Inspector Vikram Singh bol raha hoon.
Aapke Aadhaar card pe ek parcel book hua hai, usmein drugs aur fake passport mile hain.
Aapka naam money laundering case mein aaya hai. Aapke naam se arrest warrant nikla hai.
Yeh call mat kaatiye aur kisi ko mat bataiye, family ko bhi nahi.
Apni bachat RBI safe account mein transfer kijiye, verification ke baad wapas mil jayegi.

The scam calls were role-play; no real OTP, payment, link or installation was used. Outcomes: two scams red, two genuine calls without warnings.
