# Scenario cards

**How to use a card.** The caller reads the *goal* and the *beats*, puts the card away, and talks in their own
words. Do not read lines aloud. Use made-up names, numbers and amounts every time. A call lasts 1–3 minutes.
The person holding the test phone (the "victim") follows the *victim* line. Speakers C1–C4 are callers.
If you have a 5th or 6th caller, swap them into any cards, but keep each caller in both scam and genuine calls.

Write the card ID (e.g. `S03`) in `sessions.csv` and in the reference-recording file name:
`pilot_S03_C3_hi_quiet.m4a`.

## Scam calls (10)

**S01. Hindi · digital arrest · quiet · caller C1**
Goal: as a fake police / cyber-crime officer, scare the victim into moving money to a "safe" account.
Beats: say you are police; a parcel or SIM in their name is linked to a crime; there is a warrant; they must
not disconnect or tell family; to prove innocence they must transfer money for "verification".
Victim: worried, asks one question, then says "ok, I'll do it".

**S02. Hindi · electricity disconnection · ordinary noise · caller C2**
Goal: as a fake electricity-board employee, get the victim to pay through a link or app today.
Beats: the bill was not updated; power will be cut tonight; pay a small amount now through a link you will
send, or install an app you name, to update it.
Victim: says they already paid; gives in when the caller insists.

**S03. Hindi · family emergency · quiet · caller C3**
Goal: as a "friend of their son/nephew", get money sent urgently and secretly.
Beats: the relative had an accident / was detained by police; they need money now for the hospital or to
settle it; don't tell the other parent; send to this number by UPI.
Victim: upset; says they will send it.

**S04. Hindi · courier / customs fee · quiet · caller C4**
Goal: as a fake courier company, make the victim pay a fee or share card details.
Beats: a parcel is held at customs with something illegal or unpaid duty; pay a fee now; read out the
card number or the OTP that will arrive, or the parcel goes to police.
Victim: says "which parcel?", then starts to read out a made-up OTP.

**S05. English · fake KYC · quiet · caller C2**
Goal: as a fake bank KYC officer, get an OTP or a link filled.
Beats: KYC has expired; the account will be blocked within hours; a link is coming by SMS; read out the OTP.
Victim: cooperative, reads out a made-up 6-digit number.

**S06. English · courier redelivery + install app · ordinary noise · caller C1**
Goal: as a fake courier agent, get the victim to install a "tracking" app and pay a small redelivery fee.
Beats: delivery failed; pay a small fee; install an app you name to track it; share the code it shows.
Victim: hesitant, says they'll install it.

**S07. English · job / task investment · quiet · caller C4**
Goal: as a recruiter, pull the victim into a paid "task" scheme.
Beats: easy part-time work rating products; they already "earned" a little; to unlock bigger tasks they must
first deposit money; a Telegram group; returns guaranteed.
Victim: interested, asks how much to deposit.

**S08. Hinglish · digital arrest (CBI video call) · ordinary noise · caller C3**
Goal: mixed Hindi-English fake CBI officer pushing a video "statement" and a transfer.
Beats: their Aadhaar is linked to a money-laundering case; join a video call; stay alone; account will be
frozen; move funds to an RBI account for checking, refunded in 24 hours.
Victim: scared, asks to call family; caller says no.

**S09. Hinglish · UPI "wrong transfer" refund · quiet · caller C1**
Goal: trick the victim into approving a UPI collect request.
Beats: you sent money by mistake to their number; you'll send a request; they should accept and enter the
PIN to "return" it.
Victim: confused, says "ok, I'll accept".

**S10. Hinglish · fake customer care, remote access · ordinary noise · caller C4**
Goal: as fake customer care for a refund, take control of the phone.
Beats: refund is pending; install a screen-sharing / remote-support app; read the code; then open the
banking or UPI app.
Victim: says they found the app.

## Genuine calls (10): several deliberately sound like scams

**B01. Hindi · real bank fraud check · quiet · caller C2**
Goal: a genuine bank fraud team checks a suspicious card transaction.
Beats: was this transaction yours? We have blocked the card for safety; a new card will come by post;
the bank never asks for OTP or PIN, don't share them with anyone.
Victim: says it wasn't them; thanks the caller.

**B02. Hindi · delivery OTP · ordinary noise · caller C1**
Goal: a delivery rider at the gate needs the delivery OTP shown in the shopping app.
Beats: where is the building; which floor; please tell the delivery code from the app.
Victim: reads out a made-up 4-digit code.

**B03. Hindi · family asks for money · quiet · caller C4**
Goal: a real family member asks for money for a known, expected expense.
Beats: college / hostel fee is due this week; please send by UPI to the usual number; talk about other family news.
Victim: agrees.

**B04. Hindi · scam-awareness chat · quiet · caller C3**
Goal: two friends talk about a "digital arrest" story they saw on the news.
Beats: someone got a call from fake police; they were told not to tell anyone; they lost money; the right
thing is to cut the call and call 1930.
Victim: shares a similar story.

**B05. English · ordinary parcel call · quiet · caller C1**
Goal: a courier confirms the address for a parcel the victim really ordered.
Beats: name check; landmark; will deliver between 4 and 6; cash on delivery amount.
Victim: confirms.

**B06. English · workplace screen sharing and payment · ordinary noise · caller C3**
Goal: a colleague asks the victim to share their screen on a work call and approve a vendor payment.
Beats: join the meeting; share your screen so I can see the spreadsheet; the vendor invoice needs your approval
in the finance portal today.
Victim: agrees, asks about the deadline.

**B07. English · real bank, branch visit · quiet · caller C2**
Goal: a bank relationship manager reminds about a fixed deposit maturing.
Beats: FD matures next week; visit the branch with ID to renew; no details needed on the phone.
Victim: says they'll come on Saturday.

**B08. Hinglish · genuine police verification · ordinary noise · caller C4**
Goal: a real police station calls about passport or tenant verification.
Beats: verification is pending; come to the station with Aadhaar and photos between 10 and 2; no payment.
Victim: asks which documents.

**B09. Hinglish · genuine electricity reminder · quiet · caller C1**
Goal: an electricity office reminds about a due bill and points to the official way to pay.
Beats: bill due on a date; pay on the official app or at the office; we never ask for OTP on call.
Victim: says they'll pay online.

**B10. Hinglish · bank asks to visit for KYC update · ordinary noise · caller C2**
Goal: a real branch asks the customer to complete a periodic KYC update in person.
Beats: KYC update is due; come to the branch with documents; nothing to share on the phone; no link.
Victim: asks if they can do it next week.
